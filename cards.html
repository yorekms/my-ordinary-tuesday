PART I · GARAGE & WORKSHOP✂ GLOVEBOX / BENCH

High-Energy Tools, Gas Pits & Chemical Burns

  • Electrocution / Craft Rig: Never touch a shocked victim or wet bench—kill the main breaker or unplug first, then start CPR + AED.
  • Pressure-Washer / Paint Pinhole: A tiny dot with no bleeding is a surgical emergency—go straight to ER for hidden high-pressure dissection.
  • Confined Tank / Pit Collapse (H₂S / CO₂): Fading odor = lethal nerve paralysis. Never climb in to rescue without SCBA; call 911 from clean air.
  • Hydrofluoric Acid / Wet Cement: Strip soaked boots/gloves immediately, flush 20+ min with water; HF burns need ER calcium + ECG monitoring.
  • Truck Tires / Dry Ice: Never face an inflating split-rim sidewall; never carry dry ice in a shared passenger SUV cabin.
60-SEC RULE: Kill power, clear bad air, flush alkali/acid—never trust a tiny entrance wound.
PART II · KITCHEN & TABLE✂ FRIDGE DOOR

Cleaning Gases, Grill Bristles & Heat-Proof Toxins

  • Bleach Alchemy: Never mix bleach with acid toilet cleaner, vinegar, ammonia, urine, or alcohol—eyes/throat stinging? Drop the sponge and walk outside.
  • Wire Grill Brushes: Trash bristle brushes. Sharp post-BBQ throat or belly pain? Tell the ER explicitly: “We grilled recently” (plain X-rays miss thin wire).
  • Reheated Rice & Home Canning: Chill cooked rice/pasta within 2 hrs—microwave heat cannot kill cereulide. Droopy eyelids + slurred speech = ER botulism antitoxin.
  • Seafood & Honey Traps: Cold water feeling burning-hot after reef fish = ciguatera; sudden dizziness/slow pulse after raw artisanal honey = grayanotoxin ER.
60-SEC RULE: Never “finish scrubbing” in stinging fumes, and never taste-test suspect jars.
PART III · MEDICINE CABINET✂ BATHROOM MIRROR

OTC Decoys, Heat-Dumped Patches & Lying Supplements

  • Benzocaine Toothache Gel: Gray/blue lips or shortness of breath + oximeter stuck near 85%? Stop gel, call 911, ask for co-oximetry + IV methylene blue.
  • Fentanyl / Pain Patches + Heat: Never wear patches in hot baths, saunas, or under heating pads. Unresponsive? Peel patch off, give Narcan, and STILL go to ER (skin depot leaks hours).
  • Swallowed Coin Battery: ER X-ray STAT. If age >1 yr and <12 hrs, give 2 tsp honey every 10 min en route without delaying ER; never induce vomiting.
  • Biotin & Counter Traps: Tell every lab/ER about hair/nail biotin supplements (can fake thyroid labs or hide a heart attack). Skip unregulated gas-station honey/kratom.
60-SEC RULE: “No prescription needed” is a legal aisle, not a biological safety guarantee.
PART IV · GYM, TRAIL & ROAD✂ GLOVEBOX / PACK

Handlebars, Hot Vehicles, Rhabdo & Eye Traps

  • Bike Handlebar Belly Strike: Even a faint circular bruise with a calm exam can hide a crushed pancreas—demand ER trauma evaluation if pain or nausea rises.
  • Hot Cars & Vans: Never rely on habit—use a physical name-to-face roll call and visual walk-through of every row before locking doors.
  • Spin Class / Extreme Workout: Tea- or cola-colored urine + swollen crushing muscle pain = rhabdomyolysis; head to ER for IV fluids before kidneys lock up.
  • Contacts & Road Hazards: Never rinse or swim in contacts with tap/lake water (Acanthamoeba). Protect necks from taut kite lines; never yank taut string with bare hands.
60-SEC RULE: Mechanism beats early appearance—tell triage exact speed, force, and exposure.
PART V · ER FAKEOUTS✂ WALLET / PURSE

One-Finger Saves & Challenging a “Normal” First Test

  • Burst Leg Varicose Vein: Do NOT stand up or wrap a loose towel—lie flat, prop leg HIGH above heart, press ONE firm finger directly on the hole 10+ min, call 911.
  • Infant Unconsolable Crying: Strip socks, mittens, and diaper—inspect every toe/finger crease for an invisible hair-thread tourniquet; ER removes embedded loops safely.
  • Swallowing Pain + Raspy Voice: Clean tonsils and 100% oxygen do NOT rule out epiglottitis—never lie flat to test it or poke the throat; go to ER immediately.
  • When First Tests Lie: Severe pelvic pain + negative pee test (hook effect), crushing pain + normal X-ray, or sudden tearing pain + clear initial scan = ask what a repeat test or CT could miss.
60-SEC RULE: Treat the deteriorating patient, not yesterday’s reassuring printout.
PART VI · PETS (ONE MEDICINE)✂ FRIDGE / VET FOLDER

Four-Legged Roommates: Zero-Delay Vet ER Triggers

  • True Lilies & Cats: One grain of pollen, leaf bite, or vase water sip = acute kidney failure. Vet ER immediately (zero wait for vomiting).
  • Human Topicals on Pets: One pillowcase drop of minoxidil kills cats; zinc-oxide diaper cream destroys dog RBCs; dog permethrin flea drops cause feline seizures.
  • Xylitol Gum, Grapes, Sago & Slug Bait: Sugar-free xylitol crashes dog glucose fast; iron-EDTA garden bait can relapse after early improvement.
  • Puppy/Dog Dry-Heaving + Swollen Belly: Possible GDV bloat—call ahead and drive straight to a 24-hr surgical Vet ER. Rat bait? Demand Rx Vitamin K₁ (OTC K₃ fails).
60-SEC RULE: Bring the exact package/plant label to the vet ER—never “wait until morning.”
